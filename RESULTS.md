## Dataset notes
- 2026-10-26: `data/briefs/b001..b025` are ALL SYNTHETIC (generated by Claude at the user's request; the user chose not to write any briefs, marked `source: synthetic`). Their hours, costs and lessons are invented, so estimate checks against them prove consistency, not accuracy. Any metric computed on them must say so.
- 2026-10-26: eval cases `evals/cases.jsonl` (e01..e24) and `evals/retrieval_hard.jsonl` (h01..h10) were also written by Claude, the author of the corpus. Shared style and vocabulary make retrieval look easier than on independent data. The 20 human labels for the judge are the one part written by the user.
- 2026-10-27: the prompt ids stored in `evals/runs/grounded_all.jsonl` and `baseline_all.jsonl` were added after the fact (the runs were made before the registry existed). They are v1, which was verified byte-for-byte identical to the prompts used. The GitHub Actions workflows were only parsed locally as YAML; they have NOT been run on GitHub.
