## Context
Sorriso Group runs three dental clinics in the same city. Today receptionists book appointments by phone and a shared spreadsheet, and double bookings happen almost every week. The owner wants patients to book online and wants fewer no-shows. They have no in-house developers, only an agency that maintains their website.

## Scope
- Patient-facing web app: pick clinic, dentist and time slot, with confirmation by email.
- SMS reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment.
- Online deposit payment (card) for first visits.
- Receptionist dashboard with a day and week calendar, able to move or cancel appointments.
- Around 4,000 active patients, 12 dentists, 3 clinics.

## Constraints
Launch before March because the owner wants it live for the spring campaign. Patient data must stay in the country. The existing practice-management software has no API, so appointments are exported as a daily CSV file.

## Outcome
Delivered in 640 hours against an estimate of 420. The SMS provider integration took far longer than planned: delivery receipts were unreliable and we had to add a retry queue, which doubled that part of the estimate. The CSV import from the old practice-management software caused duplicate patients and cost about 60 extra hours of cleanup rules. Payments went smoothly using a hosted checkout. No-shows dropped noticeably after launch, but we did not measure by how much. Lesson: always prototype the third-party messaging integration in week one.
