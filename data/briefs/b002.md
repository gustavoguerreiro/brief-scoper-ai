## Context
Passo Certo sells shoes online and in 14 physical stores. Their online shop runs on a custom PHP system written eight years ago by a freelancer who is no longer around. It is slow during sales and nobody dares to change it. They asked us to move the shop to a modern platform without losing their search ranking.

## Scope
- Migrate catalog (about 6,000 products with sizes and colors), customers and order history.
- New storefront with the existing visual identity.
- Integration with the payment gateway and with the stock system used by the stores, so online stock matches physical stock.
- Redirects for all old product URLs.
- Admin training for the marketing team.

## Constraints
The migration had to finish before the autumn sale, with at most two hours of downtime. The stock system exposes a SOAP API that is documented only partly. Budget was fixed by the board.

## Outcome
Delivered in 900 hours against an estimate of 650. Product data was much messier than the client said: sizes were free text in the old database, and normalizing them took around 140 hours. The stock integration was the main risk and it did hurt: the SOAP API rate-limited us and stock could be 10 to 15 minutes late, which we accepted after agreeing it with the client. The cutover itself took 90 minutes of downtime. Search ranking dipped for two weeks and then recovered because of the redirect map. Lesson: audit the data quality before quoting a migration.
