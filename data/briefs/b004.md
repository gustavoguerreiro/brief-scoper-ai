## Context
A two-person startup wants to launch a personal finance app that connects to users' bank accounts, categorizes spending automatically and gives savings tips. The founders have a design in Figma and some seed money. They contacted us and three other agencies for a quote.

## Scope
- iOS and Android apps, one codebase if possible.
- Bank account connection through an open banking aggregator.
- Automatic categorization of transactions using machine learning.
- Personalized savings suggestions.
- Push notifications, a subscription paywall and an admin panel.

## Constraints
Budget around 30,000 USD and an investor demo in ten weeks. The founders want to own all the source code. Regulatory requirements for storing financial data were not discussed in the first call.

## Outcome
We did not win this one. Our quote was about twice the client's budget because we included security review, compliance work and the machine learning part. The winner offered a fixed price close to the budget with a much smaller scope. We estimated roughly 1,000 hours for the full scope but never validated that figure, since the project did not go ahead. Lesson: when scope and budget are this far apart, propose a reduced first release instead of quoting the whole wish list.
