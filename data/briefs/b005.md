## Context
A mid-size manufacturer with 900 employees onboards about 15 people a month. Onboarding is a chain of emails between HR, IT, facilities and the new hire's manager, and things get forgotten: laptops not ready, building badges missing. HR wants one place that tracks every step.

## Scope
- Checklist workflow per new hire, with tasks assigned to HR, IT, facilities and the manager.
- Automatic reminders when a task is overdue.
- Single sign-on with the company directory.
- Simple dashboard for HR showing which hires are blocked and why.
- No mobile app, desktop browser only.

## Constraints
The tool had to run on the client's own servers, with no external cloud services. Only the HR director and two analysts could be interviewed. Confidential: the client does not allow this project to be used as a public reference.

## Outcome
Delivered in 320 hours against an estimate of 300, very close to plan. The scope was small and well understood, and the single sign-on was the only technical surprise, costing about 25 hours because of the directory's custom configuration. The reminders and dashboard were done in a few days. HR reported that onboarding problems dropped, but no numbers were collected. Lesson: small internal tools with a clear process owner are predictable; the risk sits in the company's own infrastructure, not in the features.
