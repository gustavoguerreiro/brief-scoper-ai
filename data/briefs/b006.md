## Context
Vida Online is a small health insurer that wants to offer video consultations to its 30,000 members. Today members call a hotline and wait days for a callback. The medical director has already signed a contract with 40 doctors who will work remotely.

## Scope
- Member login using the insurer's existing identity system.
- Doctor availability calendar and appointment booking.
- Video consultation room in the browser, no app install.
- Post-consultation notes and e-prescriptions saved to the member record.
- Admin reports on consultations per doctor.

## Constraints
Health data privacy law applies, so all video and notes must stay in the country. Go-live was fixed for the open enrollment period in January. The insurer's identity system uses an old SAML setup.

## Outcome
Delivered in 1,100 hours against an estimate of 900. Video itself was easy using a managed video API. The overrun came from the SAML integration (about 90 hours, the insurer's test environment was down for weeks) and from compliance reviews of the prescription flow, which the client only revealed after the first demo. Load tests showed the video provider's free tier was not enough, which changed the monthly cost for the client. Lesson: ask about compliance reviews and test environments before quoting.
