## Context
A private hospital already has a patient portal built by another vendor. Patients complain they cannot see lab results online and call the lab instead. The hospital IT team wants lab results from their laboratory system to appear in the portal.

## Scope
- Read lab results from the laboratory system, which exposes HL7 messages over a TCP connection.
- Map results to the portal's REST API.
- Show results only to the patient and the ordering doctor.
- Notify the patient by email when new results are ready.

## Constraints
No changes allowed to the laboratory system. The portal vendor gave us only a short API document and a sandbox. Results must never appear before the doctor has released them.

## Outcome
Delivered in 380 hours against an estimate of 400. HL7 parsing was the known risk and was handled with an existing library. The release-by-doctor rule needed one extra status field the portal did not support, which the portal vendor added in three weeks, and we waited. Testing with realistic samples found 12 unusual message variants. Lesson: integrations with a clear interface and a cooperative owner are predictable; waiting on the other vendor is the real schedule risk.
