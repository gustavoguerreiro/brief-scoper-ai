## Context
Grão Dourado is a coffee chain with 35 stores. They run a paper stamp card and want a mobile loyalty app to see who their customers are and to send offers. Marketing owns the project and wants the first version in time for the chain's anniversary campaign.

## Scope
- iOS and Android app (cross-platform acceptable).
- Account creation, QR code per customer scanned at the register.
- Points for each purchase and rewards the customer can redeem.
- Push notifications for offers.
- Back office to create campaigns and see redemptions.

## Constraints
The registers are an old model with no API, so points have to be sent from a small agent installed on each store computer. About 25,000 customers expected in the first six months. Store staff have almost no technical training.

## Outcome
Delivered in 760 hours against an estimate of 600. The mobile app was quick. The register agent was the problem: store computers had different Windows versions and flaky internet, and we had to build an offline queue that retried sending points. That alone was around 110 hours not in the estimate. Apple's review rejected the first submission over account deletion, a two-day delay. Lesson: any software installed on the client's old store machines needs its own line in the estimate.
