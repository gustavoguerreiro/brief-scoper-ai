## Context
A home-goods retailer buys stock based on the buyers' intuition and a spreadsheet, and has regular stockouts of fast sellers and too much of slow ones. They asked for a dashboard that forecasts weekly demand by product and suggests purchase quantities.

## Scope
- Load three years of sales history from the retailer's ERP export (CSV files nightly).
- Weekly demand forecast per product and per region using standard time-series methods.
- Dashboard with forecast vs actual and suggested order quantities.
- Buyers can override a suggestion and leave a comment.

## Constraints
About 4,500 active products. The client wanted to understand the numbers, so black-box models were not accepted. Budget approved as a pilot for one product category first.

## Outcome
Delivered in 520 hours against an estimate of 500. Data cleaning took half the time: returns, duplicate product codes and promotions distorted the history. A simple seasonal model beat the more complex ones we tried on the pilot category, and the buyers understood it. Forecast error on the pilot category was lower than the buyers' own plan, but we did not measure it rigorously. Lesson: spend the first weeks on data quality; simple models are enough for a pilot.
