## Context
A distributor of cleaning products sells to around 1,800 business customers who place orders by phone, email and WhatsApp. Order entry takes three people full time. The owner wants customers to order online with their own prices.

## Scope
- Customer accounts with company-specific price lists and credit limits.
- Catalog with 3,000 products, reorder from previous orders, CSV upload of large orders.
- Approval flow for orders above the credit limit.
- Integration with the ERP to create orders and read stock and invoices.
- Invoice download and payment status.

## Constraints
The ERP is on-premises and has a vendor-provided SOAP API with an hourly request limit. Price rules are complicated: per customer, per quantity and per season. Rollout to customers in waves.

## Outcome
Delivered in 1,250 hours against an estimate of 1,000. Price rules were the surprise, with 14 exception types discovered while testing with real orders, and they took about 160 hours more than planned. The ERP request limit forced us to cache stock and prices. After launch about 40% of customers ordered online in the first three months, according to the client. Lesson: pricing logic in B2B is the real scope; get real order samples before estimating.
