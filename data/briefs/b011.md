## Context
Two founders wanted to build a marketplace where independent craftspeople sell handmade goods, with the platform taking a commission. They had a business plan, a list of 60 craftspeople interested and a small angel investment.

## Scope
- Seller onboarding and shop pages.
- Product listing, search and category browsing.
- Shopping cart with orders split by seller.
- Split payments through a marketplace payment provider, with commission.
- Reviews and messaging between buyer and seller.

## Constraints
Budget of 80,000 USD for the first release. Legal structure for handling seller payouts was not yet defined. Timeline: ten weeks to a public beta.

## Outcome
Cancelled after the discovery phase, which used 120 hours and was paid. During discovery we found that payment provider onboarding for marketplaces needs company documents the founders did not have yet, and that the commission model had tax consequences nobody had checked. The founders paused the project to deal with the legal side. The full build was never started, so the original estimate of around 900 hours was never tested. Lesson: a paid discovery phase protects both sides when legal and payment questions are open.
