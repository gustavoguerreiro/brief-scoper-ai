## Context
A distribution center with 60 pickers prepares orders using printed lists, and mistakes in picking cause about 2% of orders to be wrong. The operations manager wants handheld scanners with an app that guides each picker and verifies each item by barcode.

## Scope
- Android app for rugged handheld scanners.
- Pick lists downloaded from the warehouse management system, with route order by shelf location.
- Barcode verification, quantity confirmation and short-pick reporting.
- Works offline in dead zones of the warehouse and syncs when connected.
- Supervisor screen with picker productivity.

## Constraints
The warehouse management system offers a REST API. Scanners run an old Android version. Pilot in one aisle first, then full rollout within three months.

## Outcome
Delivered in 690 hours against an estimate of 700, on target. Offline mode was planned from the start and worked. The old Android version limited some libraries and cost about 30 hours. Pickers adopted the app quickly because the scan feedback was fast; wrong orders dropped in the pilot aisle, though we only had four weeks of data. Lesson: a clear process with a cooperative API owner is predictable; test on the real devices from week one.
