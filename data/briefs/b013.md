## Context
A freight forwarder pays about 600 carrier invoices per month. Staff compare each invoice to the agreed rate and the shipment record by hand in spreadsheets, and overcharges often go unnoticed. Finance asked for a tool that does the comparison automatically.

## Scope
- Import carrier invoices from PDF and EDI files.
- Match each invoice line to the shipment and the contract rate.
- Flag differences above a tolerance for review.
- Review screen where finance approves or disputes a line, with an export for the dispute email.

## Constraints
Eight carriers, each with a different invoice layout. Contract rates stored in several spreadsheets. Finance team of three people, none technical.

## Outcome
Delivered in 410 hours against an estimate of 300. PDF extraction of eight different layouts was much harder than expected: scanned invoices needed OCR with manual correction rules, which took around 100 hours extra. Matching rules were simple once the contract rates were put in one table. In the first quarter the tool flagged overcharges the client valued at more than the project cost, according to finance, but we did not verify the figure. Lesson: document parsing across many layouts should be estimated per layout.
