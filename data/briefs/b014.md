## Context
A regional credit cooperative processes small business loan applications on paper and email. Approval takes two weeks. The board wants an online application with document upload, credit analysis workflow and a decision trail that the regulator can audit.

## Scope
- Applicant portal: application form, document upload, status tracking.
- Analyst workspace: checklist, scoring sheet, comments and four-eye approval.
- Integration with a credit bureau API.
- Full audit log of every action and decision.
- Reports for management and for the regulator.

## Constraints
Strict regulatory requirements on data retention and audit. Credit bureau access contract had to be signed by the cooperative first. Launch planned in two phases, internal workflow first and applicant portal second.

## Outcome
Delivered in 1,600 hours against an estimate of 1,300. Audit and retention rules added roughly 150 hours: immutable logs, retention policies and a legal review of each screen. The credit bureau contract was delayed two months, so we built against a mock and found three format differences at the end. The internal workflow went live on time; the applicant portal slipped one month. Lesson: regulated domains need a compliance buffer of about 15 to 20 percent in our experience, but that figure comes from this single project.
