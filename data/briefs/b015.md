## Context
An online services company receives payments through a payment gateway and records them in an accounting ERP. Finance spends two days each month matching the gateway's settlement files to the ERP entries by hand. They want the matching automated.

## Scope
- Download daily settlement files from the payment gateway API.
- Match payments to open invoices in the ERP by reference and amount.
- Post matched payments to the ERP through its API.
- Exception list for unmatched or partial payments, reviewed by finance.

## Constraints
The ERP API is documented and has a sandbox. About 3,000 payments per month. No personal card data should be stored.

## Outcome
Delivered in 300 hours against an estimate of 320. The documented APIs and the clear matching rules made this predictable. Partial payments and refunds produced the exception cases, about 6% of payments in the first month. Finance reduced the monthly close from two days to a few hours, according to the client. Lesson: well-documented APIs and a clear rule set keep integrations on estimate.
