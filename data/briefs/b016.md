## Context
A crypto exchange wanted a new professional trading dashboard with real-time order books, advanced charting and risk tools for institutional clients. They invited several agencies to pitch and wanted the first version in five months.

## Scope
- Real-time order book and trade stream with sub-second updates.
- Advanced charting with technical indicators and drawing tools.
- Portfolio and margin risk views.
- API key management and audit trail.
- High availability across two regions.

## Constraints
Budget above 250,000 USD. Strict security review by the exchange's own team. The client asked for references with live trading products and for a team of at least eight people.

## Outcome
We did not win. The exchange chose a larger agency with previous trading-platform references. Our estimate was around 3,500 hours but it was never validated. In the pitch we had no comparable real-time trading project to show. Lesson: for specialist, high-risk domains the references decide the outcome more than the price.
