## Context
A professional training company teaches certification courses in person to about 5,000 students a year and wants its own online platform. Today they sell their courses on a third-party marketplace and pay high commissions.

## Scope
- Course catalog, checkout with subscriptions and one-off purchases.
- Video lessons, quizzes and downloadable material.
- Progress tracking and completion certificates.
- Instructor area to upload and update courses.
- Integration with the company's CRM.

## Constraints
Existing students must keep access to purchased courses. The company wants to control the video hosting cost. Launch planned for the next enrollment season in nine months.

## Outcome
We won the project and development is in progress at the time of writing. Our estimate is around 1,200 hours, not yet confirmed by actuals. The client confirmed that migration of existing student accounts is in scope, which was unclear during the pitch.
