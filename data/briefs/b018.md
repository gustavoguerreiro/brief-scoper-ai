## Context
A network of private schools tracks attendance in one system and grades in another. Teachers enter the same student lists twice, and parents get conflicting information. The schools want the two systems synchronized.

## Scope
- Synchronize student, class and teacher records from the grading system to the attendance system every night.
- Send absence notifications to parents through the attendance system's API.
- Report of records that do not match between the two systems.

## Constraints
Both vendors provide REST APIs but with different student identifiers. About 6,000 students across 9 schools. Start of the school year was a fixed deadline.

## Outcome
Delivered in 270 hours against an estimate of 250. The identifier mismatch needed a mapping table that the schools had to fill in by hand, which delayed testing by two weeks. After that the sync worked reliably. Lesson: the effort is in matching identifiers and cleaning records, not in calling the APIs.
