## Context
A manufacturer of automotive parts performs quality inspections on paper checklists at five stations. Paper gets lost and data entry later introduces errors. They want a tablet app for inspectors, with photos for defects and automatic reports.

## Scope
- Tablet app with configurable inspection checklists per part number.
- Photo capture with annotations for defects.
- Pass, fail and rework decisions with supervisor sign-off.
- Sync to a central database and daily PDF report per customer.
- Works offline on the factory floor.

## Constraints
Confidential client in the automotive supply chain; the project cannot be used as a public reference. Tablets are rugged Android devices chosen by the client. Customer audits require a traceable record for each inspection.

## Outcome
Delivered in 560 hours against an estimate of 500. Offline sync and photo storage were expected. Audit traceability needed signed timestamps and a retention policy, about 40 extra hours. Inspectors needed two short training sessions. Paper was eliminated at the pilot stations within a month. Lesson: audit-driven traceability requirements are easy to underestimate in manufacturing.
