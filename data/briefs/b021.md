## Context
A real-estate agency with 12 offices publishes listings on large portals and pays for each listing. They want their own portal to attract buyers directly and to capture leads, with listings managed by their agents.

## Scope
- Property search with map, filters and saved searches with email alerts.
- Agent area to create and edit listings with photos and floor plans.
- Lead capture and appointment request forms routed to the right agent.
- Import of existing listings from the agency's current system.
- SEO-friendly pages.

## Constraints
About 2,500 active listings. The current system exports an XML feed. Photos are large and need resizing. The agency wanted to launch before the spring buying season.

## Outcome
Delivered in 830 hours against an estimate of 800, close to plan. The map search and the photo pipeline were standard work. The XML feed had inconsistent fields between offices, so import rules were added for each office, about 50 hours. Organic traffic grew over the following months, but we did not measure the effect on leads. Lesson: map and search features are well understood; the import from the old feed is where surprises hide.
