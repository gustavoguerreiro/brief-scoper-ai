## Context
An independent group of eight boutique hotels updates room prices and availability by hand on several booking sites, and overbookings happen. They want one system that pushes availability and rates to all the sites and receives reservations back.

## Scope
- Connect to the hotels' property management system for availability.
- Push rates and availability to three online travel agencies using their partner APIs.
- Receive reservations and write them into the property management system.
- Overbooking prevention and a log of every update sent.

## Constraints
Partner API certification is required by each travel agency before going live. The property management system has a limited API. Peak season approaching.

## Outcome
Delivered in 640 hours against an estimate of 480. The certification processes were slow and each travel agency had its own test scenarios; waiting and rework added about 100 hours. Rate plans with restrictions were more complex than the documents suggested. After launch overbookings stopped, according to the group. Lesson: partner certification is a hidden schedule and effort item.
