## Context
A regional media company with a library of documentaries wants to sell monthly subscriptions to stream them online. Currently the content is on DVD and a free video site with low revenue. Management wants to launch in time for a major festival.

## Scope
- Streaming of on-demand videos on web, with a responsive player.
- Subscription plans with trial, card payments and coupons.
- Content management for the editorial team, with subtitles and thumbnails.
- Content protection to limit piracy.
- Basic analytics on views and churn.

## Constraints
The library has around 800 hours of video in different formats. Some content has regional licensing limits. About 20,000 subscribers hoped for within a year.

## Outcome
Delivered in 1,450 hours against an estimate of 1,000. Content protection was the surprise: licensors required a specific DRM level, which meant a different video platform and rework of the player, about 200 hours. Converting the library to streaming formats and fixing missing subtitles also took longer than planned. Regional licensing limits were implemented per country. The festival launch happened with a reduced catalog. Lesson: licensing and DRM requirements must be confirmed before choosing the video stack.
