## Context
A residential solar installer prepares each customer quote by hand in a spreadsheet, using roof photos and electricity bills, and a quote takes about two hours. Sales wants a tool that produces a quote in minutes during the customer visit.

## Scope
- Form to enter the roof dimensions, consumption and location.
- Estimate of system size, production and savings with configurable assumptions.
- Equipment catalog with prices maintained by the back office.
- PDF quote with the company's branding.
- Quote history and status for the sales team.

## Constraints
Confidential: pricing logic and margins must not leave the client's own accounts. Must work on tablets during visits, including with poor connectivity. Small project with a single technical contact at the client.

## Outcome
Delivered in 450 hours against an estimate of 460. The calculation logic came from the existing spreadsheet, which made requirements clear. The solar production estimate used an open dataset and was validated by the client's engineer. Offline use added around 40 hours. Quotes now take minutes, according to sales. Lesson: converting an existing spreadsheet process is predictable because the rules already exist.
