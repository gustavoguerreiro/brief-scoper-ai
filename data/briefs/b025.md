## Context
A law firm with 60 lawyers stores ten years of contracts as PDFs and Word files across shared folders. Finding a precedent clause takes hours and depends on who remembers. They want a search tool that finds contracts and clauses by meaning, not only by keywords.

## Scope
- Ingest about 40,000 documents, extract text and split into clauses.
- Search by keywords and by meaning, with filters by client, year, contract type and lawyer.
- Highlight the matching passage and link to the original document.
- Access control: lawyers see only matters they are allowed to see.
- No document leaves the firm's cloud account.

## Constraints
Confidentiality is critical. Permissions follow the firm's matter structure, which is partly recorded in a spreadsheet. Pilot with one practice group before the whole firm.

## Outcome
Delivered in 700 hours against an estimate of 600. Text extraction from scanned PDFs needed OCR and was messier than expected, around 70 hours more. The permission model took longer because the matter structure had gaps that the firm had to clean. Search quality was judged good by the pilot group in informal tests, but we did not run a formal evaluation. Lesson: access control and document cleaning take more time than the search itself.
